Data boundary

Transparent about what can and cannot be rerun from public files.

Public BAM files support read-level provenance, but the final atlas workflows require matrix-level outputs, SoupX decisions, singlet calls, cluster-review exclusions and final annotation fields. The code is therefore released as a controlled-input workflow: public-safe, reviewer-readable and runnable when the approved processed inputs are available locally.
